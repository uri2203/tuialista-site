1 What this agent does

You point it at the folder with your meeting notes or transcripts and it:

  • Reads all of them (text, Markdown, Word and PDF), without you opening them.
  • Generates structured minutes: title, date, attendees, topics, decisions and tasks.
  • Extracts tasks and commitments with their owner and due date when they appear.
  • Clearly separates decisions already made from future tasks.
  • Consolidates action items across all meetings and detects follow-ups: past tasks that might still be open.
  • Answers your questions about what was discussed: “what was agreed about the budget?”

The result: you leave every meeting with ready-made minutes and a clear list of action items, without spending time writing them.

3 How to use it

The agent opens a chat window, like a messaging app — not a programmer's black screen. You type your command or question in the text box below, hit Send (or Enter), and the reply appears as a message, just like in this real image:

The commands below are the words you can type in that same text box:

> minutes <file>

Structured minutes for that meeting: title, date, attendees, topics covered, decisions, tasks (with owner and due date) and next steps.

> tasks

Consolidates tasks from all indexed meetings, sorted by due date.

> decisions <file>

Just the concrete decisions/agreements from a meeting, without future tasks.

> follow-up

Tasks from past meetings that might still be open (no date or an overdue date), so nothing falls through the cracks.

> summary <file>

A short executive summary (max ~150 words) for anyone who missed it: what was discussed, what was decided, and the next steps.

> free-form question?

Type your question and the agent searches the meeting history, e.g.: what was agreed about the July launch?

> exit

Ends the session (exit also works).

The agent indexes the folder on startup. If you add new notes, close it and restart it so it reads them.

5 Frequently asked questions

Do my notes get uploaded to the internet?
No. Everything is processed on your computer (see Privacy, section 7).
Do I need internet to use it?
Only to validate your license (works offline for up to 3 days) and to generate minutes/summaries with AI. Reading your notes happens on your machine.
What formats does it accept?
Text (.txt), Markdown (.md), Word (.docx) and PDF (.pdf), even in subfolders.
How does it detect tasks and owners?
It combines local detection with typical phrases (“will do”, “commits to”, “pending”, “@name”, “owner:”) with an AI refinement pass.
How does it know the meeting date?
It looks for it in the first lines of the document. If it can't find it, the minutes will say “Not specified”.
What language does it reply in?
Whatever language you write to it in. Local task detection is optimized for Spanish.
Does it read a scanned PDF?
Not reliably: if it's an image with no selectable text, it won't be able to read it.
Can I change the meetings folder?
Yes. Reopen the agent and click “Choose…” to change it (if it doesn't ask, delete config.json inside %USERPROFILE%\.tuialista).
Are the minutes 100% accurate?
They're an excellent draft, but verify agreements and critical dates against your original notes before treating them as final.
Does it save history between sessions?
It keeps context while the agent is open; when you close it, that memory is lost (the files get re-indexed on restart).

6 Troubleshooting

It won't read a .docx or .pdf
Make sure the PDF has real text (not scanned). If the problem persists, contact support.
It shows “0 meetings indexed”
The folder is empty or the path is wrong. Check that you chose the right folder when activating the agent.
It says “Invalid license” or asks to reconnect
Check that the key you pasted is correct (no extra spaces), make sure you have internet the first time you activate, and if you've been offline for more than 3 days, reconnect once to revalidate.
“No AI providers configured”
Minutes, summaries and questions use AI — if the engine isn't configured, contact support. tasks can still work locally (pattern-based detection).

None of these fixed your problem? Write to us directly — we'll gladly look into it with you.

7 Privacy and confidentiality

Your meeting notes never leave your computer.

  • The agent reads and indexes the files locally, on your own machine. It doesn't upload them to any server.
  • Local detection of tasks, dates and attendees happens on your machine.
  • The only thing that travels over the internet is: (1) a check that your license is active — which doesn't include your notes' content —, and (2) when generating minutes, a summary or a question, a fragment of the text is sent to the AI engine to draft the reply.
The confidentiality of your meetings is guaranteed by design: local pattern-based task detection doesn't depend on AI. Minutes, summaries and questions do share a fragment with the AI provider so it can draft the reply.
Important reminder: this agent is a support tool, not the official record. It saves you the work of drafting and not losing action items, but its minutes are AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies.
