1 What this agent does

You point it at the contract (or the folder with your contracts) and it:

  • Explains it in everyday language: what you're signing, what you're committing to, how much you pay and for how long.
  • Detects clauses that are risky for the consumer (auto-renewal, mandatory minimum term, penalties, unilateral price hikes, hidden fees, direct debit, data sharing, high interest rates...).
  • Lists your specific obligations (what you have to do, when and how much).
  • Extracts all the costs and estimates the total cost if you complete the full contract.
  • Explains how and when you can cancel and what it costs you.
  • Suggests questions you should ask before signing.

The result: you sign actually understanding what you're agreeing to.

3 How to use it

The agent opens a chat window, like a messaging app — not a programmer's black screen. First you load the contract, then type each request in the same text box:

> load <file>

Loads the contract. Afterward you can use the other commands with that contract. E.g.: load gym_contract.pdf.

> explain <file>

The clear explanation: what the contract is, what you're committing to, what extra charges you might face, when it ends, and how to get out.

> risks <file>

Clauses that could hurt you, classified by level (HIGH / MEDIUM), with the contract fragment where they appear. It's the only action that runs 100% locally, without AI.

> obligations <file>

A specific list: what, when and how much (e.g.: “Pay $699 monthly on the 5th of each month for 12 months”).

> costs <file>

Monthly fee, signup fee, deposits, penalties, interest, tax... and the estimated total cost if you complete the full contract.

> cancel <file>

Whether you can cancel anytime or there's a minimum term, how to cancel, how much notice to give, and what they charge you.

> questions <file>

Specific questions you should ask before signing.

> free-form question?

With a contract loaded, ask whatever you want, e.g.: can they raise my monthly fee during the contract?

> exit

Ends the session (exit also works).

5 Frequently asked questions

Does my contract get uploaded to the internet?
No. It's read and analyzed on your computer (see Privacy, section 7).
What formats does it accept?
Text (.txt), Markdown (.md), PDF (.pdf) and Word (.docx).
Does this replace a lawyer?
No. It's informational support. For important contracts (large credit, mortgage, complex employment), consult a lawyer or PROFECO.
How does it detect risky clauses?
With text patterns (it looks for phrases like “automatic renewal”, “minimum term”, “cancellation penalty”...). It may miss an unusually worded clause: it doesn't replace reading it yourself.
Is the total cost it calculates exact?
It's an estimate based on the amounts it finds in the text. Verify the numbers against the contract before deciding.
Does it save my contracts?
It keeps them in memory during the session so it can answer questions. When you close the agent, that memory is erased; the files stay where you keep them.
What language does it reply in?
It's optimized for Mexican Spanish and consumer contracts in Mexico (references to PROFECO, APR, etc.).
Does it read a scanned PDF?
Only if it has selectable text. A scan that's a pure image isn't read reliably.
Can I analyze several contracts?
Yes. Load a folder with load (pointing to the directory) or pass it different files by name.
Does it modify my contract?
No. The agent only reads it.

6 Troubleshooting

It won't read a .pdf or .docx
Make sure the PDF has real text (not scanned). If the problem persists, contact support.
It says it couldn't read the contract
Check the path or the name. If you already loaded it, you can use part of the name instead of the full path.
It doesn't detect a clause I know is there
The patterns cover the most common clauses; if it's worded unusually, it might slip through. Use explain and a free-form question to check it.
It says “Invalid license” or asks to reconnect
Check that the key you pasted is correct (no extra spaces), make sure you have internet the first time you activate, and if you've been offline for more than 3 days, reconnect once to revalidate.
“No AI providers configured”
explain, obligations, costs, cancel, questions and free-form questions use AI — if the engine isn't configured, contact support. risks still works (it's local).

7 Privacy and confidentiality

Your contract never leaves your computer.

  • The agent reads and analyzes the contract locally, on your own machine. It doesn't upload it to any server or share it with anyone.
  • Detecting risky clauses (risks) happens 100% on your machine.
  • The only thing that travels over the internet is: (1) a check that your license is active — which doesn't include your contract —, and (2) for explanations and questions, a fragment of the contract (up to about 7,000 characters) is sent to the AI engine to draft the reply.
The confidentiality of your personal contracts is guaranteed by design: “risks” is 100% local. The other actions (explain, obligations, costs, cancel, questions) share a fragment of the contract with the AI provider so it can reply.
Important reminder: this agent helps you understand a contract, but it's not a lawyer. Detecting risky clauses and calculating costs is support, not a legal opinion. Before signing something important — a large loan, a mortgage, a complex employment contract — consult a lawyer or PROFECO. The final decision is yours.
