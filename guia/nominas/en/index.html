1 What this agent does

You point it at the folder with your workforce spreadsheet (Excel or CSV) and it:

  • Loads employee data, automatically detecting the columns (name, role, salary, hire date, contract type...), even under different column names.
  • Calculates the vacation balance under Mexico's Federal Labor Law (the 2023 “Fair Vacation” reform).
  • Warns you about critical dates: upcoming contract endings and work anniversaries.
  • Generates a payroll summary (total, average, by department and contract type).
  • Lists upcoming anniversaries for recognitions.
  • Alerts about contracts expiring soon.
  • Generates a headcount report (hires, departures, headcount by area).
  • Answers HR questions with anonymized context.

The result: an organized HR department, with dates under control and the data always staying home.

3 How to use it

The agent opens a chat window, like a messaging app — not a programmer's black screen. You type your command or question in the text box below, hit Send (or Enter), and the reply appears as a message, just like in this real image:

The commands below are the words you can type in that same text box:

> vacation

Per employee: their tenure, the vacation days they're entitled to (Federal Labor Law table with the 2023 reform), days taken, and days pending.

> dates [days]

HR events in the next N days (30 by default): contract endings and work anniversaries, sorted by urgency. E.g.: dates 45.

> payroll

Total payroll, average, highest and lowest salary, and a breakdown by department and contract type — without unnecessarily exposing sensitive individual data.

> anniversaries [days]

Employees with a work anniversary in the next N days (60 by default).

> contracts [days]

Temporary contracts expiring in the next N days (45 by default), including recently expired ones.

> headcount

Total headcount, active employees, departures, hires this year, and a breakdown by department and contract type.

> free-form question?

Type any question in natural language, e.g.: how many employees hit 5 years this quarter?

> exit

Ends the session (exit also works).

5 Frequently asked questions

Does my employee data get uploaded to the internet?
No. It's loaded and processed on your computer (see Privacy, section 7).
What formats does it accept?
Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV (.csv). The agent reads the workforce data from spreadsheets, not from PDF or Word.
Do I need to name my columns exactly?
No. The agent automatically detects columns with various names in Spanish and English (name/employee, salary/wage, hire date...). It needs at least the name or salary column to recognize the file.
How does it calculate vacation time?
With Mexico's Federal Labor Law table and the “Fair Vacation” reform (2023): 12 days in the first year, rising to 20 by the fifth; from the sixth year on, 22 days plus 2 more for every additional 5 years.
Is the vacation calculation legally binding?
No. It's an estimate of the legal minimum based on tenure. Verify it with your legal team before approving; your company may grant more days.
Does it expose individual salaries?
The summary and question-answering are designed to respect privacy and not expose sensitive individual data unless necessary.
What language does it reply in?
Whatever language you write to it in. The vacation calculation is based on Mexican legislation.
Does it modify my employee file?
No. The agent only reads it.
I updated the spreadsheet, does it pick it up?
Restart the agent; it reloads the data from the folder.
Can I have several files?
Yes. It loads every .xlsx/.xls/.csv in the folder and its subfolders.

6 Troubleshooting

It won't load employees / says “0 employees”
Check that you chose the right folder when activating the agent, and that the file has a recognizable name or salary column in the first row.
It misreads a column
Rename the header to a more common variant (for example, “Name”, “Salary”, “Hire date”) and restart.
The vacation calculations don't match what's expected
The agent calculates the legal minimum by tenure; your company may grant more. Check with your legal team.
It says “Invalid license” or asks to reconnect
Check that the key you pasted is correct (no extra spaces), make sure you have internet the first time you activate, and if you've been offline for more than 3 days, reconnect once to revalidate.
“No AI providers configured”
Only free-form questions use AI — if the engine isn't configured, contact support. dates, vacation, payroll, anniversaries, contracts and headcount still work locally, without AI.

7 Privacy and confidentiality

Your employee data never leaves your computer.

  • The agent loads and processes the workforce data locally, on your own machine. It doesn't upload employee data to any server.
  • The vacation calculation, payroll reports, critical dates and headcount are all calculated on your machine.
  • The only thing that travels over the internet is: (1) a check that your license is active — which doesn't include employee data —, and (2) when asking a free-form question, an anonymized context (aggregate summaries, without exposing individual salaries by default) is sent to the AI engine to draft the reply.
The confidentiality of your personnel data is guaranteed by design: vacation, payroll, critical dates and headcount are 100% local. Only free-form questions share anonymized context with the AI provider.
Important reminder: vacation, benefits and tax calculations are estimates; validate them with your legal/accounting team. The agent calculates the legal minimum vacation under the Federal Labor Law (2023 reform) based on tenure, but every company may offer better benefits and laws change. Before approving vacation, severance, or contract renewals, confirm with your legal or accounting team.
