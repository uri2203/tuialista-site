1 What this agent does

You point it at the folder with your documents (IDs, proofs, certificates, official records) and it:

  • Reads and classifies them by type (voter ID, national ID number, tax ID, proof of address, birth certificate, etc.).
  • Generates a checklist for the procedure: which requirements you already have and which are missing.
  • Detects documents about to expire or already expired (for example, a proof of address older than 3 months).
  • Produces a step-by-step guide for the procedure (government office, requirements, whether it can be done online, costs and approximate times).
  • Answers your questions: “what do I need to renew my passport?”

The result: you show up to do the paperwork with everything in order and no surprises.

3 How to use it

The agent opens a chat window, like a messaging app — not a programmer's black screen. You type your command or question in the text box below, hit Send (or Enter), and the reply appears as a message, just like in this real image:

The commands below are the words you can type in that same text box:

> documents

A summary of your documents, their detected type, and their status (valid / expiring soon / expired).

> checklist <procedure>

For a procedure (e.g. passport, voter id, tax id), which requirements you already have (and if any is expired) and which ones you're missing.

> guide <procedure>

Full guide: government office, documents, whether it can be done online, steps, tips to avoid rejection, costs and approximate times.

> missing <procedure>

Just what you're missing for that procedure, and whether any document is expired.

> expirations

Documents about to expire or already expired.

> procedures

Catalog of procedures the agent knows (passport, voter ID, tax ID, national ID number, driver's license, social security, birth certificate, tax certificate, e.signature...).

> free-form question?

Type any question in natural language, e.g.: can I get my national ID number online?

> exit

Ends the session (exit also works).

5 Frequently asked questions

Do my documents get uploaded to the internet?
No. Everything is processed on your computer (see Privacy, section 7).
What formats does it accept?
Text (.txt), Markdown (.md), PDF (.pdf) and Word (.docx), even in subfolders.
Does it do the paperwork for me or book it?
No. The agent prepares and guides you: classifies your documents, builds the checklist and gives you the step-by-step guide. You do the actual procedure at the government office or portal.
Does it generate official documents?
No. It prepares the guide and checklist of documents you need and detects which ones you already have; it doesn't generate official documents.
How does it classify my documents?
By keywords and patterns (e.g. “voter ID”, “national ID number”, “utility bill”, “tax ID”...). It can misclassify an unusual document.
Is the paperwork information always up to date?
The agent ships with a reference catalog, but requirements change. That's why it always reminds you to verify on the official government website.
What language does it reply in?
Whatever language you write to it in. The catalog is focused on Mexican paperwork.
Does it read a scanned PDF?
Only if it has selectable text. A scan that's a pure image isn't read reliably.
Can I change the folder?
Yes. Reopen the agent and click “Choose…” to change it (if it doesn't ask, delete config.json inside %USERPROFILE%\.tuialista).
Is this official government information?
No. It's a TuIAlista support tool, not an official channel. Confirm everything on the government office's website.

6 Troubleshooting

It won't read a .pdf or .docx
Make sure the PDF has real text (not scanned). If the problem persists, contact support.
A procedure doesn't show up
Use procedures to see the available catalog. If the name doesn't match, try a variant (e.g. “voter id”, “national id”).
It shows “0 documents indexed”
The folder is empty or the path is wrong. Check that you chose the right folder when activating the agent.
It says “Invalid license” or asks to reconnect
Check that the key you pasted is correct (no extra spaces), make sure you have internet the first time you activate, and if you've been offline for more than 3 days, reconnect once to revalidate.
“No AI providers configured”
guide and free-form questions use AI — if the engine isn't configured, contact support. documents, checklist, missing, expirations and procedures still work locally, without AI.

None of these fixed your problem? Write to us directly — we'll gladly look into it with you.

7 Privacy and confidentiality

Your personal documents never leave your computer.

  • The agent reads and classifies the files locally, on your own machine. It doesn't upload them to any server or share them with any government office.
  • Classification, the checklist, and expiration detection all happen on your machine.
  • The only thing that travels over the internet is: (1) a check that your license is active — which doesn't include your documents —, and (2) when generating a guide or asking a question, a fragment (document type and your query) is sent to the AI engine to draft the reply.
The confidentiality of your ID documents is guaranteed by design: classification, the checklist and expirations are 100% local. Only “guide” and free-form questions share a fragment with the AI provider.
