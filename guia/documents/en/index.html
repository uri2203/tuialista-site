1 What this agent does

It's the simplest agent in the catalog. You point it at the folder where you keep your documents and it:

  • Reads all of them (PDF, Word, text and Markdown), without you opening them one by one.
  • Answers your questions in plain language: “what does the manual say about vacation time?”
  • Cites the file it got each fact from, so you can verify it.
  • If the answer isn't in your documents, it tells you instead of making something up.

The result: you find any fact in seconds, without remembering which file it was in.

5 Frequently asked questions

Do my documents get uploaded to the internet?
No. Everything is processed on your computer (see Privacy, section 7).
Do I need internet to use it?
Only for two things: validating your license (works offline for up to 3 days) and generating replies with AI. Reading and indexing your documents happens on your machine.
What file formats does it accept?
PDF, Word (.docx), text (.txt) and Markdown (.md). It searches even inside subfolders.
Can it read a scanned PDF?
Not reliably. It works best with PDFs that have real, selectable text.
What language does it reply in?
Whatever language you write to it in.
How many documents can it handle?
There's no fixed limit; it comfortably handles folders with dozens of documents.
Are the answers 100% accurate?
It's very useful, but with long or unusually formatted documents it can miss a nuance. Use it as a guide and verify anything critical in the original document it cites.
Can I change the documents folder?
Yes. Reopen the agent and click “Choose…” to change it (if it doesn't ask, delete config.json inside %USERPROFILE%\.tuialista).
Does it save a history of my questions?
It keeps conversation context while it's open, to understand follow-up questions. When you close it, that memory is lost.
Does it replace a search engine or document manager?
It's a complement: it understands natural-language questions and gives you the answer, not just a list of files.

6 Troubleshooting

It won't read a PDF / shows “PDF not read”
The PDF is probably a scanned image with no real text. Try a version with selectable text.
It says “Invalid license” or asks to reconnect
Check that the key you pasted is correct (no extra spaces), make sure you have internet the first time you activate, and if you've been offline for more than 3 days, reconnect once to revalidate.
“No AI providers configured” when asking a question
Answers use AI. If you see this message, the AI engine isn't configured; contact support.
It shows “0 documents indexed”
The folder is empty or the path is wrong. Check that you chose the right folder when activating the agent.

7 Privacy and confidentiality

Your documents never leave your computer.

  • The agent reads and indexes the files locally, on your own machine. It doesn't upload them to any server or share them with anyone.
  • Searching and selecting the relevant chunks happens on your machine.
  • The only thing that travels over the internet is: (1) a check that your license is active — which doesn't include your documents' content —, and (2) when you ask a question, a fragment of the relevant documents is sent to the AI engine to draft the reply.
The confidentiality of your information is guaranteed by design: the product exists precisely so you can query your documents without exposing them. Every question shares only the relevant fragment with the AI provider — never the whole folder.
