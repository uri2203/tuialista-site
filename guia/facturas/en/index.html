1 What this agent does

You point it at the folder with your invoices (SAT CFDI XML and/or PDF) and it:

  • Captures each invoice into an organized Excel — what's normally done by hand in hours: invoice number, vendor, tax ID, dates, subtotal, VAT, withholding, total and currency.
  • Summarizes the period's taxes, adding up what each invoice declares by currency (base, tax, withholding, total).
  • Builds your accounts payable with alerts for what's overdue and what's due soon.
  • Detects duplicate invoices.
  • Answers any question or request about your invoice portfolio in your own words — no commands to memorize.

The capture and calculation work follows fixed, already-tested rules, on your computer (no AI, no making things up): it reads CFDI XML exactly and any PDF invoice by reading its text. The result: you pay on time, without duplicating, with your invoice Excel ready without typing it in yourself.

3 How to use it

The agent opens a chat window, like a messaging app — not a programmer's black screen. You don't memorize exact commands: you write what you need almost like you'd ask a person, hit Send (or Enter), and it does the work. These are the 4 main things it knows how to do, with examples of how to ask:

> export  /  generate the Excel  /  capture them

Builds you an Excel with everything: capture of each invoice, tax summary and accounts payable, ready to open.

> taxes  /  how much VAT

Tax summary for the period: base, tax, withholding and total, added up by currency.

> what's due  /  what's expiring  /  how much do I owe

Your accounts payable, most urgent first: overdue, due soon, and current, with vendor and amount.

> duplicates  /  repeated invoices

Detects invoices that appear to be loaded twice (same invoice number/UUID, or same vendor and total).

> any other question

E.g.: “how much do I owe ACME”, “total by vendor”, “compare this month against last month” — the agent understands the request and builds the answer or report.

You don't need to hit an exact word: “invoice due”, “what's due this week” or “accounts payable” all trigger the same thing. If your phrase doesn't match any of the 4 main actions, the agent interprets it with AI and still tries to help you.

5 Frequently asked questions

Do my invoices get uploaded to the internet?
No. They're read and processed on your computer (see Privacy, section 7).
What formats does it accept?
CFDI XML (SAT, Mexico) and invoice PDF, plus .txt. The XML is read exactly (not a text guess); the PDF is read by its content. It doesn't read Word, Excel or CSV as input — the output is an Excel file, though.
Do I need to type the exact command?
No. The agent recognizes intent by keywords (for example “export”, “excel”, “capture” and “generate” all trigger the same thing). You can phrase it however comes naturally.
Does it detect duplicates reliably?
It flags possible duplicates (same UUID/invoice number, or same vendor and total). Confirm them manually before rejecting or canceling a payment.
Does it execute payments?
No. The agent organizes, captures and alerts; it doesn't make or schedule payments. You execute them in your bank or system.
What language does it reply in?
Whatever language you write to it in.
Does it read a scanned PDF?
Only if it has selectable text. A scan that's a pure image isn't read reliably — for those cases, the CFDI XML is the safest source.
Does it modify my original invoices?
No. The agent only reads them; the Excel it generates is a new file, separate from your invoices.
Can I change the folder?
Yes. Reopen the agent and click “Choose…” to change it (if it doesn't ask, delete config.json inside %USERPROFILE%\.tuialista so it asks again).
What is “safe mode”?
By default the agent only uses already-tested tasks (export, taxes, payables, duplicates, and some automatic reports) — never AI writing code over your data. If you type “expert mode”, it also tries new requests with AI (isolated, it can't touch your files). Go back with “safe mode”.

6 Troubleshooting

It won't read a file
Make sure it's .xml (CFDI), .pdf or .txt — other formats aren't read as input. If it's a PDF, confirm it has real text (not scanned).
It says it didn't find any invoices
The folder is empty or the path is wrong for this file type. Check that you chose the right folder when activating the agent, or use “Folders…” to point to where they are.
It doesn't detect a piece of invoice data correctly
The PDF may have an uncommon format. CFDI XML is more reliable than the PDF because it's read exactly, not by text.
It says “Invalid license” or asks to reconnect
Check that the key you pasted is correct (no extra spaces), make sure you have internet the first time you activate, and if you've been offline for more than 3 days, reconnect once to revalidate.
The AI isn't responding to a free-form question
“export”, “taxes”, “what's due” and “duplicates” always work, with no connection to an AI engine. Only questions outside those 4 actions depend on AI — if it fails, retry in a moment or contact support.

7 Privacy and confidentiality

Your invoices never leave your computer.

  • The agent reads and processes invoices locally, on your own machine. It doesn't upload them to any server.
  • The Excel capture, tax summary, accounts payable and duplicates are calculated on your machine, with fixed, already-tested rules (no AI).
  • The only thing that travels over the internet is: (1) a check that your license is active — which doesn't include your invoices —, and (2) if you ask for something outside the 4 main actions (a free-form question), a relevant fragment of the information is sent to the AI engine so it can reply.
The confidentiality of your financial information is guaranteed by design: export, taxes, accounts payable and duplicates are 100% local, with fixed, already-tested rules — no AI. Only free-form questions outside those 4 actions share a fragment of data with the AI provider so it can reply.
Important reminder: verify the extracted data before making a real payment. CFDI XML is read exactly, but PDFs are read by text and in unusually formatted invoices a piece of data can get confused. Duplicate detection is an alert, not a certainty. Before paying, canceling or rejecting an invoice, confirm the data against the original document. The agent organizes and alerts; the payment decision is yours.
