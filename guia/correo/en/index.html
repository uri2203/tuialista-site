1 What this agent does

You point it at the folder with your exported emails and it:

  • Reads all of them (.eml, .mbox, .txt and .csv), with no connection to mail servers.
  • Prioritizes each message: urgent, important, normal or low.
  • Extracts the tasks being asked of you, who's asking and by when.
  • Detects likely unanswered items.
  • Drafts professional reply drafts in the language of the email.
  • Groups by sender and searches within your emails.
  • Answers your questions about the inbox: “what was I asked for with a deadline this week?”

The result: you stop wasting time on your inbox and act first on what actually matters.

3 How to use it

The agent opens a chat window, like a messaging app — not a programmer's black screen. You type your command or question in the text box below, hit Send (or Enter), and the reply appears as a message, just like in this real image:

The commands below are the words you can type in that same text box:

> priority

Your inbox sorted by urgency: URGENT, IMPORTANT, NORMAL, LOW. Flags the ones with a deadline.

> summary

Executive overview of the inbox: how many urgent/important, most frequent senders, and what to handle first.

> tasks

Emails asking for an action, who's asking, the priority, and the deadline mentioned if there is one.

> reply <subject>

A reply draft for the email you specify (by subject or number), in the language of the original, with blanks [IN BRACKETS] for you to fill in.

> unanswered

Emails asking for action whose sender doesn't appear as a recipient of another email of yours — possibly unanswered.

> senders

Groups your emails by sender, sorted by volume.

> search <text>

Searches for a word or phrase in the subject and body. E.g.: search invoice march.

> free-form question?

Type any question in natural language, e.g.: what urgent emails do I have from this week?

> exit

Ends the session (exit also works).

5 Frequently asked questions

Do my emails get uploaded to the internet?
No. Everything is processed on your computer; your emails never leave your machine (see Privacy, section 7).
Does it connect to my Gmail/Outlook account?
No. The agent does not connect to mail servers. It works on files you export yourself (.eml, .mbox, .txt, .csv).
Do I need internet to use it?
Only to validate your license (works offline for up to 3 days) and for summaries, drafts and AI questions. Reading and prioritizing emails is local.
How does it decide priority?
By keywords (urgent, asap, today, deadline, important, please, confirm...) and by detecting deadline dates. It's a very useful guide, not a foolproof rule.
What language does it draft replies in?
In the language of the original email. If they write to you in Spanish, the draft comes out in Spanish.
How does it detect “unanswered” items?
With a heuristic: emails asking for action whose sender doesn't appear as a recipient in another one of your emails. There can be false positives; use it as a hint.
Can I change the email folder?
Yes. Reopen the agent and click “Choose…” to change it (if it doesn't ask, delete config.json inside %USERPROFILE%\.tuialista).
Does it read attachments (PDF, Word)?
No. The agent processes the body of the emails, not their attachments.
Does it modify or send emails?
No. It only reads and drafts drafts; it never sends anything or modifies your real inbox.
Does it save history between sessions?
It keeps context while it's open; it's lost when you close it (it re-indexes on restart).

6 Troubleshooting

It shows “0 emails”
The folder is empty or the path is wrong. Check that you chose the right folder when activating the agent, and that it has .eml, .mbox, .txt or .csv files.
A .csv doesn't read well
Make sure it has recognizable column headers (from/to/subject/body).
It says “Invalid license” or asks to reconnect
Check that the key you pasted is correct (no extra spaces), make sure you have internet the first time you activate, and if you've been offline for more than 3 days, reconnect once to revalidate.
“No AI providers configured”
summary, reply and questions use AI — if the engine isn't configured, contact support. priority, tasks, senders and search still work locally, without AI.

7 Privacy and confidentiality

Your emails never leave your computer.

  • The agent reads and processes the files locally, on your own machine. It doesn't connect to your mail server or upload your emails anywhere.
  • Prioritization, task detection and grouping by sender all happen on your machine.
  • The only thing that travels over the internet is: (1) a check that your license is active — which doesn't include your emails' content —, and (2) when requesting a summary, a draft, or a question, a fragment of the relevant emails is sent to the AI engine to draft the reply.
The confidentiality of your correspondence is guaranteed by design: priority, tasks, senders and search are 100% local. Only summary, reply and free-form questions share a fragment with the AI provider.
Important reminder: this agent is a productivity support tool. It prioritizes, summarizes and drafts replies, but its classifications and replies are AI-generated and can be wrong. Always review a draft before sending it, and confirm yourself what's actually urgent.
