What does it solve and what do you need?

Inventory is a balancing act: run short and you lose sales; overstock and you tie up cash. Checking this by hand in spreadsheets is tedious and almost nobody does it on time. This agent turns your inventory files into a purchasing and capital control panel.

  • You need: your license key (TIA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) and the folder with your inventory files (Excel or CSV). No Python, no technical knowledge.
  • First result in under 5 minutes: install, activate, pick your folder, and run low to see which product is about to run out.
  • Your business data never leaves your computer — the analysis runs locally.
Notice: this agent is a decision-support tool, not an automatic purchasing system. Its suggestions are based on simple rules over the data you give it; verify critical figures before buying or writing off inventory.

1 What this agent does

You point it at the folder with your inventory files and it:

  • Reads them (Excel .xlsx and CSV), automatically recognizing the columns (in Spanish or English).
  • Detects products below minimum stock and their urgency.
  • Suggests how much to reorder for each product (based on its turnover, or a default value).
  • Calculates the total value of inventory, at cost and at selling price, by category and vendor.
  • Identifies dead stock (no movement) that ties up capital.
  • Classifies products with ABC analysis (where the value is concentrated).
  • Detects excess inventory.
  • Answers your questions: “what should I reorder first?”

The result: you know what to buy, what to free up, and where your money is, in seconds.

3 How to use it

The agent opens a chat window, like a messaging app — not a programmer's black screen. You type your command or question in the text box below, hit Send (or Enter), and the reply appears as a message, just like in this real image:

The commands below are the words you can type in that same text box:

> low [threshold]

Products with stock at or below their minimum (or the threshold you give), sorted by urgency (CRITICAL / URGENT / LOW) with estimated days left if there's turnover data. E.g.: low 5.

> reorder

For each low product, suggests how much to order (based on ~30-day turnover, or a default value) and estimates the cost.

> value

Total value at cost and at selling price, potential margin, and breakdown by category and vendor.

> dead [days]

Products with stock but no movement in the last N days (90 by default), sorted by locked-up value. E.g.: dead 120.

> abc

A/B/C (Pareto) classification by inventory value: the “A” group concentrates most of the value and deserves the most attention.

> excess

Products with stock well above their minimum and the capital tied up in that excess.

> free-form question?

Type any question in natural language, e.g.: which beverage-category products are about to run out?

> exit

Ends the session (exit also works).

5 Frequently asked questions

Does my data get uploaded to the internet?
No. Everything is processed on your computer (see Privacy, section 7).
What formats does it accept?
Excel (.xlsx) and CSV (.csv). Old .xls files are best saved as .xlsx.
Do I need to name my columns exactly?
No. The agent recognizes many names (for example, “stock”, “quantity”, “qty” for inventory count; “sale price”, “retail price”; “unit cost”...), in Spanish and English. Still, clear column names improve results.
Does it really “predict” when to reorder?
It makes a rule-based estimate: it uses your daily turnover (if it's in the data) to calculate how long the stock will last and how much to order for ~30 days; if there's no turnover data, it uses a default value. It's not a machine-learning prediction model.
Do I need the turnover or last-sale-date column?
They're not required, but they improve results: without turnover, days left and some suggestions are more generic; without “last sale date”, dead stock is marked as “no date”.
What language does it reply in?
Whatever language you write to it in.
Does it modify my files?
No. The agent only reads your data.
Can I change the data folder?
Yes. Reopen the agent and click “Choose…” to change it (if it doesn't ask, delete config.json inside %USERPROFILE%\.tuialista so it asks again).
Are the calculations exact?
They're done locally on your data. Still, verify critical figures and make sure your cost/price/stock columns are captured correctly.
Can it read several files?
Yes. It loads every .xlsx and .csv in the folder.

6 Troubleshooting

It says “CSV/XLSX with no recognized columns”
Your file doesn't have a clear product/SKU or stock column. Check the headers.
It won't load a .xlsx
Make sure the file is a valid .xlsx. If the problem persists, contact support.
It says “Invalid license” or asks to reconnect
Check that the key you pasted is correct (no extra spaces), make sure you have internet the first time you activate, and if you've been offline for more than 3 days, reconnect once to revalidate.
“No AI providers configured”
Free-form questions use AI — if the engine isn't configured, contact support. low, reorder, value, dead, abc and excess still work locally, without AI.

7 Privacy and confidentiality

Your business data never leaves your computer.

  • The agent reads and analyzes your files locally, on your own machine. It doesn't upload them to any server.
  • All calculations (low stock, value, ABC, excess, dead stock) happen on your machine.
  • The only thing that travels over the internet is: (1) a check that your license is active — which doesn't include your data —, and (2) when you ask a free-form question, a relevant inventory summary is sent to the AI engine to draft the reply.
The confidentiality of your costs, prices and stock levels is guaranteed by design: all calculations (low stock, reorder, value, ABC, excess, dead stock) are 100% local. Only free-form questions share a summary with the AI provider so it can reply.
Important reminder: this agent is a decision-support tool, not an automatic purchasing system. Its reorder suggestions and analysis are based on the data you give it and simple rules. Verify critical figures (costs, prices, stock) and apply your business judgment before buying or writing off inventory.
